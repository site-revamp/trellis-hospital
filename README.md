Trellis Hospital Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
Trellis Hospital is a private multi-specialty tertiary care hospital located in Jumeirah, Dubai, UAE. Formerly known as Gargash Hospital, it is part of Trellis Health, a healthcare network owned by Al Ghurair. The hospital provides specialist consultations, emergency care, surgical procedures, diagnostic services and comprehensive medical treatment.

The redesigned homepage focuses on translating the hospital's Connected Caring philosophy into a more patient-focused digital experience. It prioritises appointment access, simplifies specialty discovery and strengthens the presentation of clinical expertise and hospital credibility.
2. Main Improvements
1. More Prominent Patient Actions
- Reworked the hero section to give patients clearer access to essential actions.
- Prioritised Book an Appointment, Find a Doctor and Explore Specialties.
- Balanced the Connected Caring brand message with practical patient needs.
Problem addressed: The previous homepage placed greater emphasis on brand messaging than helping visitors quickly access appointments, doctors and medical services.
2. Improved Medical Specialty Discovery
- Organised medical specialties into clearer, patient-friendly categories.
- Improved the presentation of departments and their associated services.
- Created more direct pathways for visitors to explore relevant medical treatments.
Problem addressed: The hospital's broad range of specialties lacked sufficiently intuitive organisation, making it harder for patients to identify the right department.
3. Stronger Trust and Clinical Expertise Presentation
- Improved the presentation of consultant profiles and professional qualifications.
- Gave greater visibility to patient feedback, hospital credentials and clinical capabilities.
- Integrated trust-building information into the patient journey.
Problem addressed: Clinical expertise, patient feedback and hospital credentials were not presented cohesively enough to support patient confidence when choosing a hospital.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: Established a professional healthcare-focused appearance aligned with Trellis Hospital's Connected Caring positioning.
- Homepage Layout: Improved content hierarchy to prioritise patient actions, medical specialties and clinical credibility.
- User Experience: Simplified access to appointments, doctor discovery and department information.
- Content Consistency: Structured specialty information and trust-building content into a more cohesive presentation.
- Conversion Focus: Increased the visibility of appointment and doctor discovery pathways to help patients take the next step.

Project Focus: Patient-focused navigation, easier specialty discovery and stronger clinical credibility.
